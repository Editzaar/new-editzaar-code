Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with Editzaar for creative production and growth.

01. How does the process work after I contact Editzaar? +

We start with a quick discussion to understand your goals, requirements, references, and timeline. Once the scope is clear, we share the plan and pricing. After approval, our team begins the work and keeps you updated through the process.

02. Can I see your previous work before hiring you? +

Absolutely. You can explore our portfolio and previous projects to see our quality, creative approach, and experience before making a decision. We can also share relevant work based on your specific requirements, so you know exactly what to expect.

03. How many revisions do I get? +

Every project includes a defined number of revisions based on the selected package. We first understand your requirements carefully to reduce unnecessary back-and-forth, and our goal is to make sure the final output matches the agreed creative direction.

04. Do you work on both one-time projects and monthly requirements? +

Yes. We offer project-based services for specific requirements as well as monthly partnerships for businesses that need consistent support. This includes video editing, graphic design, website development & SEO, paid advertising, and social media management. Browse our transparent packages.

05. How do I know my project will be handled professionally? +

Your project is handled by a dedicated team with a defined workflow, clear deliverables, and agreed timelines. We maintain structured communication throughout the project so you always know what's being worked on, what's next, and when to expect delivery.
